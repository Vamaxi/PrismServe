# Mixed-power diagnostic study

This complete initial M10 session has 60 GPU traffic runs, 12 CPU/CUDA portability runs, matched prediction probes and eight 2048-target quality arms. Its own validation and source snapshot are retained.

The laptop changed from battery to AC during the GPU series. Observed steady-joint clocks and throughput changed substantially, so these mixed-condition measurements are diagnostic and are not the primary controlled comparison. The AC-conditioned final study is in `results/validation_study_final/`.
